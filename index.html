What you'll see

Places we visit

A closer look at each stop across the tours above.

Little Adam's Peak

Easy 45-min climb with sweeping views over tea estates.

Nine Arch Bridge

Colonial-era railway bridge in the jungle, best seen around train times.

Rawana Ella (Falls)

A wide roadside waterfall, one of the most photographed on the island.

Asapuwa

A small hilltop temple with quiet views over the Ella valley.

Secret Waterfall

A lesser-known waterfall off the main road, away from the crowds.

Ella Wala

A local spot along the route, good for a short stop and photos.

Ravana Cave

A hillside cave tied to the Ramayana legend, reached by a short climb.

Uda Diyaluma

Natural rock pools above Diyaluma Falls, with wide valley views.

Lipton Seat

Sir Thomas Lipton's viewpoint, with panoramic views over the tea estates.

Ella Rock

A longer hike to one of the best viewpoints over the Ella gap.
